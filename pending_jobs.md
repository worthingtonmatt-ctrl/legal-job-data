The Associate General Counsel is responsible for providing a high level of professional legal service by
representing and advising the Arizona Board of Regents and Arizona State University on a broad
range of legal matters in the higher education sector.
This position is within the ASU Office of General Counsel (OGC) transactional practice group, which
concentrates on a variety of complex transactional matters including complex commercial
transactions, business formation and structures, global and national operations, finance, including
public finance and other legal matters. The group provides legal advice and counsel on state and
federal laws, applicable regulations, and Arizona Board of Regents (ABOR) and ASU policies impacting
the university; assists with the development of educational training programs for administrators,
faculty, and staff; manages a significant and varied workload including working on multiple unrelated
matters concurrently and completing complex projects both independently and with minimal
direction; utilizes excellent judgment and discretion as well as exceptional integrity and research and
communication skills.
